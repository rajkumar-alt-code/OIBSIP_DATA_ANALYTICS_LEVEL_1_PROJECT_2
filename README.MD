# 📊 Customer Behavior & Marketing Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

🚀 An end-to-end **Business Intelligence & Data Analytics project** built using **Excel and Power BI**, focused on converting raw customer and marketing data into **clear, actionable business insights**.

This project reflects **real-world BI practices** followed by experienced data analysts — emphasizing clean data preparation, thoughtful KPI design, and executive-ready dashboard storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary goals of this analysis are to:
- Understand customer spending behavior
- Evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Analyze purchase channel preferences
- Support data-driven business decisions

The dashboards are designed to be **decision-focused, clutter-free, and scalable**.

---

## 🧾 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains customer-level information including:
- Demographics: Age, Education, Marital Status, Income
- Product-wise spending across multiple categories
- Purchase behavior across different sales channels



---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and validation  
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, KPI creation, dashboard development  
- **DAX** – Measure-based KPI logic  
- **GitHub** – Project hosting and portfolio presentation  

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation Approach

Data preparation was performed in Excel to ensure:
- Removal of invalid and missing income values
- Validation of numeric spending columns
- Consistent binary campaign indicators
- Clean, analysis-ready structure

No artificial data or unnecessary columns were created.

---

## 📐 Data Modeling Philosophy

- KPIs are created using **measures**, not static columns
- Multiple campaign response columns are logically aggregated
- Product-level analysis is handled through **summary-level aggregation**
- Visual clutter is minimized by avoiding row-level overload

This approach aligns with **industry-standard BI modeling practices**.

## 🔎 Key Insights

- Higher-income customers generally exhibit higher total spending
- A small segment of customers contributes a large share of revenue
- Store and web channels dominate overall purchase behavior
- Campaign responses are concentrated among specific customer groups

This analysis enables organizations to:
- Improve campaign targeting and personalization
- Focus efforts on high-impact customer segments
- Optimize product and channel strategies
- Strengthen data-driven decision-making
